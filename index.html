Questions.

Will this get my account blocked? +

Any tool that sends DMs carries some risk. LeadOS keeps it low: a warm-up for new accounts, a daily limit, minutes between messages, and sending from your own browser.

Is it spam? +

You approve who gets a message, AI skips people who don't fit, and every first DM is written for that person. It's a handful of messages a day.

Can it really find people in my city? +

It looks where local people show up: posts from your area's gyms and parks, followers of the gyms near you, your city's hashtags.

Does my computer need to be on? +

Yes, while a campaign runs. Messages go out from your own Chrome.

What's a founding spot? +

The first 15 trainers on the waitlist get 7 days of Starter free, then $49 a month instead of $69, for as long as they stay subscribed.

What if the 15 spots are gone? +

The waitlist stays open, and you'll get in at the normal price.
