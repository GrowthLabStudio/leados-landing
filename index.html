Questions

Straight answers

Will this get my account banned?

Every tool that sends DMs carries some risk, so LeadOS is built to keep it low: new accounts warm up first, there's a daily limit, minutes between messages, and messages go from your own browser instead of a server.

Is this spam?

No. You choose who gets a message, AI filters out people who don't fit, and every first DM is personal. A few good messages a day beat hundreds of copies.

Can it find people in my city?

Yes. LeadOS looks where local people already show up: who posts from your area's gyms and parks, who follows the gyms near you, and who uses your city's hashtags.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, while a campaign runs. LeadOS sends from your own Chrome, which is a big part of what keeps your account safe.

What's a founding spot?

The first 15 trainers on the waitlist get 7 days of Starter free, then pay $49 a month instead of $69, for as long as they stay subscribed.

What happens after the 15 spots are gone?

The waitlist stays open. You'll get early access at the normal price.
