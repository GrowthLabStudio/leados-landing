We tried it by hand first

Before we built anything, we spent three days sending cold DMs ourselves. Two things stood out.

Finding the right people took far longer than writing to them. And one of our accounts sent too many messages too fast, so Instagram blocked its DMs for about four days.

So we built a tool that does the slow part, and does it carefully.

What it does

It looks where local people already show up: who posts from the gyms and parks in your area, who follows the gyms near you, who uses your city's hashtags.

AI reads each profile against what you offer and drops the people who don't fit.

You approve a personal first message. LeadOS sends it from your own Instagram. If there's no answer, it follows up. When someone replies, it stops, and the conversation is yours.

Everyone you've messaged lands in a simple CRM, so nobody gets forgotten.

About your account

LeadOS sends from your own Chrome, not from a server. It keeps to a daily limit and waits minutes between messages. If your account is new, it warms up first.

Any tool that sends DMs carries some risk. We'd rather send you fewer messages than get you blocked.
