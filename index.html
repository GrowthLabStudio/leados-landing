Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Will this get my account banned?

Every tool that sends DMs carries some risk, so LeadOS is built to keep it low: a warm-up period before you send, a daily limit, minutes between messages, and sending from your own browser instead of a server.

Is this spam?

No. You choose who gets a message, AI filters out people who don't fit, and every first DM is personal. A few good messages a day beat hundreds of copies.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Yes, while a campaign runs. LeadOS sends from your own Chrome, which is a big part of what keeps your account safe.
