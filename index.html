What it does

Finds people near you

It looks where local people already show up: who posts from the gyms and parks in your area, who follows the gyms near you, who uses your city's hashtags, who comments and likes.

Checks who's a fit

AI reads each profile against what you offer, so you only message people who might actually want a trainer.

Sends the first message

A personal DM you've approved goes out from your own Instagram. If they don't answer, it follows up. When they reply, it stops and hands the chat to you.

Keeps track

Every person you've messaged sits in a simple CRM, so you can see who's new, who replied and who's ready to book.

Careful with your account

Send too many DMs too fast and Instagram can block your messages for days. So LeadOS sends a few a day, minutes apart, from your own Chrome, never from a server. If your account is new, it warms up before anything goes out.

Questions

Will this get my account blocked?

Any tool that sends DMs carries some risk. LeadOS keeps it low: a daily limit, minutes between messages, a warm-up for new accounts, and sending from your own browser.

Is it spam?

You pick who gets a message, AI skips people who don't fit, and every first DM is written for that person. It's a handful of messages a day.

Does my computer need to be on?

Yes, while a campaign runs. Messages go out from your own Chrome.

Is it only for personal trainers?
